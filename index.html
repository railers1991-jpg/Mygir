Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. You can create unlimited invoices and download PDFs with no watermark and no sign-up. Paid Pro only removes the saved-document and company limits for registered users.

Do I need an account?

No. The generator works without signing up — your data stays in your browser. Create an account only if you want to save invoices to the cloud, manage multiple companies, or use the dashboard.

Where is my data stored?

Without an account, everything stays on your device. If you sign in, your invoices and clients are stored securely in your private cloud space, visible only to you.

Can I send invoices to clients?

Yes. Save an invoice while signed in and share a link — your client can open it in their browser and download the PDF.
